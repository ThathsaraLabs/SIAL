FOR SRI LANKANS IN SOUTH KOREA

KIIP Preparation Course

Prepare for the Korea Immigration and Integration Program (KIIP) with SIAL. The course is designed for Sri Lankans in South Korea, including E-9 workers exploring the E-7-4 skilled worker pathway.

  • Build your Korean

    Practise Korean for daily life and work through vocabulary, reading and conversation.

  • Prepare for KIIP

    Study Korean language and society topics and ask SIAL which preparation class suits your current level.

  • Understand the E-7-4 pathway

    Get guidance on where to check language evidence and the current skilled worker criteria. Immigration authorities assess eligibility and decide applications.

Ask SIAL about class availability, fees and enrollment. For official KIIP registration and visa requirements, use the Korean immigration resources below.
